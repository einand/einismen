          <h1>Reasoning and logic</h1>
          <p class="lead">Einismen does not only try to assert ideas. It also tries to show how they are motivated and why certain conclusions become reasonable.</p>

          <figure class="image-break">
            <img src="../../../media/detail-logic-hero.svg" alt="">
            <figcaption>Steps, derivation and sustainable conclusions.</figcaption>
          </figure>

          <p>Ideas are motivated by returning to consistency, sustainability and real function. If a viewpoint makes humanity clearer, stronger and more consistent, it has greater value than what only sounds right in theory.</p>
          <p>Conclusions are drawn through analysis, comparison and testing. Opposing ideas are allowed to meet so weaknesses become visible. Therefore dialectical improvement becomes part of the reasoning: it is through resistance that a stronger synthesis can emerge.</p>
          <p>Common arguments in Einismen therefore revolve around function, integrity, responsibility, testability and the ability to continue improving over time.</p>
