# Power and leadership

{{< lead >}}Leadership is also about who gets to choose and who must live with the consequences.{{< /lead >}}

{{< hero-image >}}

## The responsibility of power

Power influences decisions, resources and other people’s room to act. It may come from a role, but also from knowledge, money or social influence.

## Efficiency and harm

A system can be efficient and still cause harm. Results must be weighed against dignity, health and fair distribution.

## Review and transparency

Power needs transparency, feedback and correction. Criticism must be heard; authority does not make itself infallible.

## Leadership as stewardship

Responsible leadership treats power as held in trust. It asks whose voices are missing and how harmful decisions can change.
