          <p>In everyday life, Einismen means distinguishing between what can be influenced and what must be accepted. In work, it means choosing function over prestige and improving step by step instead of clinging to routine.</p>
          <p>In relationships, Einismen means words should be backed by action, boundaries should be clear and trust should not be built on ambiguity. In society, it means robust, comprehensible and testable systems are valued higher than symbolic facades.</p>
          <p>The practical value lies in the same principles being applicable at multiple levels: clarity, responsibility, integrity and continuous improvement.</p>
