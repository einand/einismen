# Care and relationships

{{< lead >}}People are shaped through relationships. A philosophy of freedom and responsibility must also ask how we care for one another.{{< /lead >}}

{{< hero-image >}}

## Care as an action

No person comes into being alone. We learn trust and boundaries with others, and sometimes depend on care and support.

## Boundaries and reciprocity

Care appears in attention, time and practical help. It can require difficult conversations and clear boundaries; caring does not mean always agreeing.

## Relationships and integrity

Reciprocity does not require perfect balance each day. Needs change, but relationships need room for both giving and receiving over time.

## Carrying responsibility together

Integrity and care can support each other. Einismen must see people as both independent and vulnerable, relying on trust and shared responsibility.
