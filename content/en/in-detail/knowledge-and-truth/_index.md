# Knowledge and truth

{{< lead >}}Seeking truth begins by separating what we observe, the conclusions we draw and what we hope is true.{{< /lead >}}

{{< hero-image >}}

## Reasons and evidence

An observation is what we examine; an interpretation is the explanation we give it. Ask what is known, assumed and what information could change the view.

## Reconsidering a view

A belief is not well founded merely because it feels obvious. Examine the evidence, alternative explanations and whether the conclusion survives resistance.

## Decisions under uncertainty

If new evidence weakens a view, reconsider it. This does not mean accepting every new claim at once; sources may be uncertain or lack context.

## Without a claim to infallibility

We often act without complete knowledge. State what is known, show the reasons and mark uncertainty. Truth does not require infallibility, but readiness to correct oneself.
