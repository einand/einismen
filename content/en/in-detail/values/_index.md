          <h1>Values</h1>
          <p class="lead">What is valued most in Einismen is clarity, integrity, function, responsibility and the will to improve what can be improved.</p>

          <figure class="image-break">
            <img src="../../../media/detail-values-hero.svg" alt="">
            <figcaption>What carries weight and is prioritized.</figcaption>
          </figure>

          <p>What is seen as important is what helps humanity live more clearly and consistently: to understand better, stand firm when needed, speak truer and act so that words and actions point in the same direction.</p>
          <p>What is seen as wrong is above all that which breaks apart direction: manipulation, shirking responsibility, self-deception, routine and empty prestige. Einismen does not judge by facade but by effect and consequence.</p>
          <p>What is strived for is not perfection but a life where the individual becomes more true to their own stated values and where both the person and the systems they participate in are refined over time.</p>
