# Objections to Einismen

{{< lead >}}Einismen emphasizes clarity, responsibility and function. It must be tested against the risk that these values simplify people and their circumstances.{{< /lead >}}

{{< hero-image >}}

## The limits of rationalism

Demands for strength can be harsh toward people facing illness, vulnerability or few choices. Responsibility must be distinguished from blame without room to act.

## Individualism and dependence

Feelings carry information about needs and harm. They do not decide truth alone, but should not be dismissed as interference.

## Function for whom?

Freedom can obscure people’s dependence on one another and shared institutions. Yet community can demand obedience. Both sides must remain visible.

## Questions that remain open

When function is a measure, ask: for whom and at what cost? The philosophy’s credibility depends on admitting open questions, hearing criticism and changing when reasons require it.
