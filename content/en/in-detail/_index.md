## Overview

\`In detail\` is divided into subpages that follow the main parts of the philosophy. Here you can move from a broad picture to more focused explanations of [origin](about-the-philosophy), [values](values), [ethics](ethics-and-principles), [application](practical-application), [logic](reasoning-and-logic), [examples](examples) and [criticism](criticism-and-objections).

If you want more connected and declarative texts instead, go to [Texts](../texts). \`In detail\` exists to orient, compare and understand each area on its own.
