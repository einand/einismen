          <h1>In detail</h1>
          <p class="lead">Here Einismen is gathered as a reference map. `In detail` breaks the philosophy down topic by topic instead of presenting it as longer primary texts.</p>
        </section>
        <figure class="image-break"><img src="../../media/en/detail-hero.svg" alt=""><figcaption>A map of parts that together form the whole.</figcaption></figure>
        <section class="section prose">
          <h2>Overview</h2>
          <p>`In detail` is divided into subpages that follow the main parts of the philosophy. Here you can move from a broad picture to more focused explanations of <a href="about-the-philosophy">origin</a>, <a href="values">values</a>, <a href="ethics-and-principles">ethics</a>, <a href="practical-application">application</a>, <a href="reasoning-and-logic">logic</a>, <a href="examples">examples</a> and <a href="criticism-and-objections">criticism</a>.</p>
          <p>If you want more connected and declarative texts instead, go to <a href="../texts">Texts</a>. `In detail` exists to orient, compare and understand each area on its own.</p>
        </section>
