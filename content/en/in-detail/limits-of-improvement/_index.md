# The limits of improvement

{{< lead >}}Improvement matters when it serves a real purpose. It becomes harmful when people are reduced to things that must always be optimized.{{< /lead >}}

{{< hero-image >}}

## Function and human worth

The call to develop can become a demand for everything to be faster and more productive. Then we forget who pays the cost.

## Rest and preservation

People’s worth does not depend on performance. A more efficient practice can still harm health, trust or dignity.

## What should change?

Rest can support sound judgment. Preservation can protect knowledge, community and stability that change would destroy.

## A measure of improvement

Ask what problem a change solves and who benefits. Improvement may mean doing less, preserving what works or refusing a solution whose costs are too high.
