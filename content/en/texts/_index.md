        <section class="section prose">
          <h2>What Einismen tries to do</h2>
          <p>Texts on Einismen often need to be longer than an ordinary introduction, because the philosophy moves between several layers at once. It is both a basic view of the world, an ethical stance, a method of analysis and a way of working with improvement, which is also reflected in <a href="../in-detail">In detail</a> and in the page on <a href="../practice">Practice</a>.</p>
          <p>That means it does not quite fit inside simple labels. Einismen is not only a theory of meaning, and not only a method of efficiency. It tries to hold together the human being's inner stance with their outward actions. That is why some parts need to be described in greater detail, especially in <a href="basics">The basics of Einismen</a> and <a href="method">Function, method and improvement</a>.</p>
          <p>In the texts gathered here, the longer lines are brought together: how Einismen views the uncertainty of reality, why <a href="../pillars/freedom">freedom and responsibility</a> must belong together, why <a href="../pillars/weight-of-words">the weight of words</a> returns, and why improvement is not seen as optional but as part of a dignified life.</p>
          <p>If you instead want a more systematic walkthrough divided by subject areas, continue to <a href="../in-detail">In detail</a>. There the content is structured more like a reference work than a collection of primary texts.</p>
