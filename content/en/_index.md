## What makes Einismen distinct

Einismen tries to hold together levels that are often separated: worldview, character, ethics, work and method. It does not only want to describe the world. It also wants to give a person a way of living more consistently within it.

What distinguishes it is that it settles for neither comfort nor technique. It asks a person to see reality more clearly, carry more responsibility for chosen directions and build stronger ways of living inside uncertainty.

The site therefore moves between short introductions and deeper explanations. You can begin simply and then continue into the more systematic layers when you want a fuller picture.
