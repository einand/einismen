        <section class="section prose">
          <h2>What makes Einismen distinct</h2>
          <p>Einismen tries to hold together levels that are often separated: worldview, character, ethics, work and method. It does not only want to describe the world. It also wants to give a person a way of living more consistently within it.</p>
          <p>What distinguishes it is that it settles for neither comfort nor technique. It asks a person to see reality more clearly, carry more responsibility for chosen directions and build stronger ways of living inside uncertainty.</p>
          <p>The site therefore moves between short introductions and deeper explanations. You can begin simply and then continue into the more systematic layers when you want a fuller picture.</p>
