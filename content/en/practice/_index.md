        <section class="section prose">
          <h2>Why gather this under Practice</h2>
          <p>These pages belong close together. Everyday life, work and ethics are different fields, but Einismen tries to use the same basic principles in all three: clarity, responsibility, function, <a href="../pillars/weight-of-words">the weight of words</a> and directed improvement.</p>
          <p>That is why `Practice` works as a natural hub. It makes it clearer that the philosophy is not only meant to be thought about, but also to be carried out, something developed further in <a href="../in-detail/practical-application">Practical application</a>.</p>
          <p>In <a href="../everyday-life">everyday life</a> the principles appear as habits, patience and responsibility. In <a href="../work-and-leadership">work</a> they appear as function, iteration and better decisions. In <a href="../ethics-and-conflicts">ethics</a> they appear as clear boundaries, the weight of words and the willingness to act honorably even when situations are difficult.</p>
        </section>
