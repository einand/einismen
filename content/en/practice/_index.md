## Why gather this under Practice

These pages belong close together. Everyday life, work and ethics are different fields, but Einismen tries to use the same basic principles in all three: clarity, responsibility, function, [the weight of words](../pillars/weight-of-words) and directed improvement.

That is why \`Practice\` works as a natural hub. It makes it clearer that the philosophy is not only meant to be thought about, but also to be carried out, something developed further in [Practical application](../in-detail/practical-application).

In [everyday life](../everyday-life) the principles appear as habits, patience and responsibility. In [work](../work-and-leadership) they appear as function, iteration and better decisions. In [ethics](../ethics-and-conflicts) they appear as clear boundaries, the weight of words and the willingness to act honorably even when situations are difficult.
