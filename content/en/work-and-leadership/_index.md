          <div class="span-6"><h3>Pragmatism</h3><p>Methods are not sacred. If something works better, it should replace the old.</p></div>
          <div class="span-6"><h3>Iteration</h3><p>Small adjustments often beat major restarts.</p></div>
          <div class="span-6"><h3>Leadership</h3><p>A leader should be clear, sober and able to change when the evidence improves.</p></div>
          <div class="span-6"><h3>Cooperation</h3><p>Independence is valuable. The right cooperation is often better.</p></div>
