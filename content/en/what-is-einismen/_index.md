          <h1>What is Einismen?</h1>
          <p class="lead">Einismen begins from the thought that the world does not hand us finished answers. A person must think for themselves, choose direction and stand by those choices.</p>
          <p>It is both a life stance and a method. It tries to unite realism with responsibility, <a href="../pillars/freedom">freedom</a> with consequence and <a href="../pillars/change">change</a> with reflection.</p>

          <div class="span-6">

              <li>A method for better decisions.</li>
              <li>A support for continual refinement.</li>
            </ul>
