# What is Einismen?

{{< lead >}}Einismen begins from the thought that the world does not hand us finished answers. A person must think for themselves, choose direction and stand by those choices.{{< /lead >}}

{{< hero-image >}}

It is both a life stance and a method. It tries to unite realism with responsibility, [freedom](../pillars/freedom) with consequence and [change](../pillars/change) with reflection.

{{< /section >}}

{{< section class="split" >}}

{{< box class="span-6" >}}

<span class="mini-label">Not this</span>

- Not blind belief.
- Not ready-made answers to everything.
- Not empty tradition.
- Not change for its own sake.

{{< /box >}}

{{< box class="span-6" >}}

<span class="mini-label">Closer to this</span>

- A clear way of thinking.
- A disciplined way of living.
- A method for better decisions.
- A support for continual refinement.
