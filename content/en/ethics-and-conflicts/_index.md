          <h1>Ethics and conflicts</h1>
          <p class="lead">Einismen does not search for perfect rules. It searches for clarity, responsibility and boundaries that work.</p>
        </section>
        <figure class="image-break">
          <img src="../../media/en/ethics-hero.svg" alt="">
          <figcaption>Boundaries, judgment and steady restraint.</figcaption>
        </figure>
        <section class="section split">
          <div class="span-4"><h3>Ethics</h3><p>What matters is consequence, responsibility and real effect.</p></div>
          <div class="span-4"><h3>Morality</h3><p>A person must think for themselves and carry their judgments.</p></div>
          <div class="span-4"><h3>Communication</h3><p>Be clear and sober even when others are not.</p></div>
          <div class="span-6"><h3>Conflict</h3><p>Do not choose conflict for its own sake. Do not avoid necessary boundaries either.</p></div>
          <div class="span-6"><h3>Manipulation</h3><p>See it early. Stay calm. Do not surrender clarity.</p></div>
