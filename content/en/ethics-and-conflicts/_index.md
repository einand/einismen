# Ethics and conflicts

{{< lead >}}Einismen does not search for perfect rules. It searches for clarity, responsibility and boundaries that work.{{< /lead >}}

{{< hero-image >}}

{{< /section >}}

{{< section class="split" >}}

{{< box class="span-4" >}}

### Ethics

What matters is consequence, responsibility and real effect.

{{< /box >}}

{{< box class="span-4" >}}

### Morality

A person must think for themselves and carry their judgments.

{{< /box >}}

{{< box class="span-4" >}}

### Communication

Be clear and sober even when others are not.

{{< /box >}}

{{< box class="span-6" >}}

### Conflict

Do not choose conflict for its own sake. Do not avoid necessary boundaries either.

{{< /box >}}

{{< box class="span-6" >}}

### Manipulation

See it early. Stay calm. Do not surrender clarity.

{{< /box >}}
