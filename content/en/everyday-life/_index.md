          <div class="span-4"><h3>Habits</h3><p>Build small habits that last longer than big promises.</p></div>
          <div class="span-4"><h3>Stress</h3><p>Separate what you can influence from what you must accept.</p></div>
          <div class="span-4"><h3>Relationships</h3><p>Be clear. Keep your word. Avoid unnecessary fog.</p></div>
          <div class="span-6"><h3>Responsibility</h3><p>Your background matters, but your next choice is still yours.</p></div>
          <div class="span-6"><h3>Balance</h3><p>You do not need to control everything. You need to know what deserves your energy.</p></div>
