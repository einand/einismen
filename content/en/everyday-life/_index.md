# Einismen in everyday life

{{< lead >}}Focus on what you can influence. Release what you cannot control. Choose the next clear step.{{< /lead >}}

### Habits

Build small habits that last longer than big promises.

{{< /box >}}

{{< box class="span-4" >}}

### Stress

Separate what you can influence from what you must accept.

{{< /box >}}

{{< box class="span-4" >}}

### Relationships

Be clear. Keep your word. Avoid unnecessary fog.

{{< /box >}}

### Responsibility

{{< /box >}}
