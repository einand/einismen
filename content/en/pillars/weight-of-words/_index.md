          <h1>Let words carry weight</h1>
          <p class="lead">Einismen treats words as binding. They gain gravity only when carried by action, responsibility and openness.</p>
          <figure class="image-break"><img src="../../../media/en/questions-hero.svg" alt=""><figcaption>Words create direction only when action keeps them alive.</figcaption></figure>
          <p>Words are not only a way of expressing thought. They build expectation, create or break trust and shape direction. When someone speaks carelessly, both the person and the relations around them weaken.</p>
          <p>This pillar does not mean that a person may never change. It means that change must happen openly. If a promise cannot be kept, that must be said. If a position shifts, it must be acknowledged.</p>
          <p>Respect for the weight of words makes Einismen honor-bound. Integrity becomes not a feeling but a relation between speech, action and consequence.</p>
