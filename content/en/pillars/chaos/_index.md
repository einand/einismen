          <h1>Accept chaos</h1>
          <p class="lead">Chaos means uncertainty and a world without ready-made answers. Einismen begins by accepting this.</p>

          <figure class="image-break">
            <img src="../../../media/detail-origin-hero.svg" alt="">
            <figcaption>Chaos is the starting point, not the enemy.</figcaption>
          </figure>

          <p>Einismen takes its starting point in reality, not in wishful thinking. And reality is rarely orderly. The world bears no ready-made meaning, no guarantee of justice, no assurance that the one who strives will be rewarded.</p>
          <p>This is not a pessimistic claim. It is an honest one. Chaos means that everything is in motion, that nothing is fixed, and that humans cannot rely on anyone else to arrange their existence for them.</p>
          <p>Whoever accepts chaos avoids one thing: disappointment that the world does not live up to expectations. Instead, they can use their energy to act within what actually exists, rather than what ought to exist.</p>
