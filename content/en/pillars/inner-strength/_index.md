          <h1>Build inner strength</h1>
          <p class="lead">When outer order cannot be guaranteed, inner steadiness becomes all the more important. That is why Einismen makes the virtues practical.</p>

          <figure class="image-break">
            <img src="../../../media/en/horizon-lines.svg" alt="">
            <figcaption>Steadiness is built not from a secure facade but from inner direction.</figcaption>
          </figure>

          <p>Inner strength is not about hardness for its own sake. It is about being able to remain in clarity when circumstances shift. Wisdom, courage, self-discipline, integrity and reflection therefore return as central tools.</p>
          <p>Wisdom makes it possible to distinguish between what can be influenced and what must be accepted. Courage allows the human being to act despite fear. Self-discipline allows them to hold direction over time. Integrity keeps them from drifting apart between word and action. Reflection allows them to learn instead of merely reacting.</p>
          <p>This pillar makes Einismen less dependent on outer luck. It does not say that the human being can control everything, but that they can build a stance that makes them less easy to divide.</p>
