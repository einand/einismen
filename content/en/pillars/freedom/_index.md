          <h1>Live freely with responsibility</h1>
          <p class="lead">Freedom in Einismen is not loose or sentimental. It is the right to shape your own life, but also the duty to bear what your choices create.</p>

          <p>This pillar refuses to reduce a person to tradition, habit or obedience. Each person has the right to define values and direction for themselves.</p>
          <p>But freedom cannot become an excuse for denying consequence. Freedom without responsibility turns empty. Responsibility without freedom turns into force. Einismen keeps the two in tension.</p>
          <p>To live freely therefore means being authentic without becoming careless. A person should be able to say: this is my path. But they must also be able to stand for what that path does to themselves and to others.</p>
