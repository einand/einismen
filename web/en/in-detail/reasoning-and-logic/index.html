Reasoning and logic

Einismen does not only assert ideas. It also tries to show how they are argued for and why certain conclusions become reasonable.

The ideas are justified by returning to consequence, durability and real function. If a view makes a person clearer, stronger and more consistent, it has more value than something that only sounds correct in theory.

Conclusions are drawn through analysis, comparison and testing. Opposed ideas are allowed to meet so their weaknesses become visible. That is why dialectical improvement becomes part of the reasoning.

Common arguments therefore circle around function, integrity, responsibility, testability and the capacity to keep improving over time.
