Reasoning and logic

Einismen does not only try to assert ideas. It also tries to show how they are motivated and why certain conclusions become reasonable.

Ideas are motivated by returning to consistency, sustainability and real function. If a viewpoint makes humanity clearer, stronger and more consistent, it has greater value than what only sounds right in theory.

Conclusions are drawn through analysis, comparison and testing. Opposing ideas are allowed to meet so weaknesses become visible. Therefore dialectical improvement becomes part of the reasoning: it is through resistance that a stronger synthesis can emerge.

Common arguments in Einismen therefore revolve around function, integrity, responsibility, testability and the ability to continue improving over time.
