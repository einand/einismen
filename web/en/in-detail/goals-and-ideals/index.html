Goals and ideals

Einismen does not aim at comfort as its highest good. It aims at clearer lives, stronger character and better forms of living and working.

The good life here is not passive ease. It is a life in which a person sees reality more clearly, chooses more honestly and becomes more capable of carrying consequence.

The ideals are therefore tied to integrity, courage, discipline, responsible freedom and the ability to keep learning. The goal is not purity but refinement.

At the level of systems, the ideal is similar: structures should become more understandable, more honest and more functional over time.
