Ethics and principles

Einismen does not offer endless rulebooks, but it does point clearly toward how a person should act if they want to live with integrity.

A person should act so that their words can be carried by their deeds. They should be clear, responsible and open to changing when better understanding appears. They should not choose the convenient over the true merely to avoid friction.

Einismen offers guidelines rather than rigid laws: choose function without abandoning ethics, protect your freedom without despising that of others, and set boundaries against what breaks clarity, trust and direction.

Relation to others should be sober, respectful and clear. That does not mean weakness, but it does mean that boundaries should be set with judgment rather than impulse.
