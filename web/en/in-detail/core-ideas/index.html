Core ideas

Einismen rests on several recurring assumptions: reality is unstable, the individual must orient themselves, and truth requires testing rather than comfort.

Its worldview begins in uncertainty rather than fixed harmony. Human beings are not given a finished place but must shape direction under pressure, limitation and incomplete knowledge.

The philosophy also assumes that knowledge grows through observation, reasoning and testing. Claims that cannot survive resistance should not be treated as secure.

Because of this, Einismen gives a central place to responsibility. A person is not reduced to circumstance, but neither are circumstances ignored.
