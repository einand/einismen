Values

What is valued most in Einismen is clarity, integrity, function, responsibility and the will to improve what can be improved.

What matters most is whatever helps a person live more clearly and more consistently: understanding better, standing firm when needed, speaking more truthfully and acting so that words and deeds point in the same direction.

What is treated as wrong is above all what breaks direction: manipulation, evasion of responsibility, self-deception, inertia and empty prestige. Einismen does not judge by surface but by effect and consequence.

What is sought is not perfection but a life in which a person becomes more faithful to their stated values and the systems they inhabit become more refined over time.
