Values

What is valued most in Einismen is clarity, integrity, function, responsibility and the will to improve what can be improved.

What is seen as important is what helps humanity live more clearly and consistently: to understand better, stand firm when needed, speak truer and act so that words and actions point in the same direction.

What is seen as wrong is above all that which breaks apart direction: manipulation, shirking responsibility, self-deception, routine and empty prestige. Einismen does not judge by facade but by effect and consequence.

What is strived for is not perfection but a life where the individual becomes more true to their own stated values and where both the person and the systems they participate in are refined over time.
