Examples

Abstract principles become clearer when they are applied. Examples show how Einismen reads ordinary dilemmas and practical choices.

One example is a promise that becomes hard to keep. Einismen does not demand frozen loyalty to the impossible. It demands honesty, openness and responsibility for what was spoken.

Another example is a failing system at work. The philosophy does not prefer prestige or habit. It asks what functions, what can be tested and what can be improved without illusion.

Examples matter because they keep the philosophy from floating away into slogans.
