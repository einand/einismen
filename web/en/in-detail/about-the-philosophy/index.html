About the philosophy

Einismen grows out of the need for a philosophy that does not hide the world's uncertainty and still gives a person direction, steadiness and tools for choosing.

The background is an experience that reality is often more contradictory than the systems that claim to explain it. Where other outlooks offer finished comfort, Einismen begins in the admission of chaos, limitation and responsibility.

The philosophy exists to give a way of living that does not depend on denial. It tries to join realism with direction, freedom with responsibility and clarity with the ability to keep developing.

It does not want to remain theory. It wants to affect how a person thinks, speaks, works and relates to others.
