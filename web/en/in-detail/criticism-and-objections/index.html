Criticism and objections

Einismen can sound hard, strict or overly rational. These are reasonable objections and they need to be met openly.

A common criticism is that the emphasis on function can sound cold or instrumental, and that the demand for integrity and responsibility can become heavy for people already living under pressure.

Einismen replies that clarity is not the same as coldness and function is not the same as indifference. The philosophy tries to protect a person from illusions that in the long run weaken them.

Still, the weakness remains: Einismen can become too severe if it loses its human tone. That is exactly why its own principles also need repeated testing.
