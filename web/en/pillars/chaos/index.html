Pillars / Chaos

Accept chaos

Einismen begins with a simple but demanding insight: the world is not built to feel simple, fair or finished for the human being.

Accepting chaos does not mean giving up. It means stopping the demand that reality should fit our wishes before we are willing to live within it. Only when uncertainty is acknowledged can the human being begin to choose for real.

In Einismen this pillar means that good and evil are not treated as simple absolute blocks that always arrive pre-sorted. They appear in a world where consequences, perspectives and responsibility must be understood. That is why clear-sightedness is required instead of reflex.

Chaos is also the ground of freedom. If the world does not deliver ready-made answers, the human being must create direction for themselves. That makes life heavier, but also truer. Chaos therefore becomes not only a threat but a condition for something truly chosen to arise.
