Pillars / Change

Improve further

Einismen does not treat a person or a system as finished. That is why learning and change become a necessary pillar, not an optional extra.

No human being is final, and no system is perfect. Because of that, learning cannot be left to chance. What can be improved should be improved.

Change does not mean overturning everything all the time. Change should be conscious, understandable and directed toward refinement. What works can be refined. What no longer works must be reconsidered.

This pillar keeps Einismen alive. Without it the philosophy would harden into doctrine instead of remaining a method for clearer living.
