Introduction

What is Einismen?

Einismen begins from the thought that the world does not hand us finished answers. A person must think for themselves, choose direction and stand by those choices.

It is both a life stance and a method. It tries to unite realism with responsibility, freedom with consequence and change with reflection.

Not this
  • Not blind belief.
  • Not ready-made answers to everything.
  • Not empty tradition.
  • Not change for its own sake.
Closer to this
  • A clear way of thinking.
  • A disciplined way of living.
  • A method for better decisions.
  • A support for continual refinement.
