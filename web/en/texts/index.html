What Einismen tries to do

Texts on Einismen are often longer than an ordinary introduction because the philosophy moves through several layers at once. It is a worldview, an ethical stance, a method of analysis and a way of working with improvement.

That means it does not fit neatly inside simple labels. Einismen is not only a theory of meaning and not only a method of efficiency. It tries to hold together inner stance and outward action.
