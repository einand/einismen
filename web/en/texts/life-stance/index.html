Life stance, words and mortality

Einismen is not only a theory about the world. It is also a way of inhabiting life with more steadiness, honesty and seriousness.

One of the clearest lines in the material is respect for the weight of words. When a person speaks, they should do so with responsibility. Words are not only sounds. They create trust, expectation and direction.

Another line is the need for inner strength. Without self-discipline, reflection and courage, a person becomes too dependent on changing moods and external noise.

Mortality also sharpens the philosophy. Death is not denied or softened away. The fact that life ends is part of what makes responsibility, attention and direction matter.
