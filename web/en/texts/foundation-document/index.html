Foundation document

This is a connected foundation document for Einismen, written from the collected material. It holds together the philosophy's pillars, method, freedom, responsibility and view of life in one longer form.

Einismen begins by admitting that the world is not arranged for our comfort. Because of that, a person must learn to see clearly and choose direction without leaning on comforting illusion.

The philosophy rests on five recurring pillars: acceptance of chaos, inner strength, freedom joined to responsibility, the weight of words and continual improvement. They do not stand as isolated advice. They support one another.

It also places strong emphasis on method. Arguments should be tested, systems refined and criticism used to create stronger forms rather than only to tear down.

In that sense Einismen is not merely contemplative. It aims at clearer lives, stronger character and better structures.
